### Selected work

**[TTM Shipping & Accessories](https://ttmshipping.com)** · `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `Vercel`
A live shipping-management platform for a Jamaican courier forwarding packages from the USA and China. Marketing site, authenticated customer portal, staff admin dashboard, and an inbound courier webhook feed. Per-user data isolation via Postgres Row Level Security, transactional email, and an immutable audit log on every mutation.

**Hemophilia Society of Jamaica** · `React` `Vite` `Tailwind` `GitHub Actions`
A five-page awareness and education site for a health nonprofit. Built on a centralized design-token system, WCAG 2.1 AA compliant down to the ARIA tab patterns and reduced-motion fallbacks, with content isolated into data files so non-developers can maintain it.

**[Tech By Tahjay](https://ikarudo.github.io/TechByTahjay/)** · `HTML` `CSS` `Vanilla JS`
My portfolio, plus a gallery of four hand-coded demo sites built to show range: a recipe site with a live servings scaler, a blog that darkens as you scroll, a hot-sauce storefront that re-skins itself by heat level, and a villa booking flow with a working calendar range picker. No frameworks, no animation libraries.

**Scribit** · `React Native` `Expo` `TypeScript` `Firebase`
A cross-platform student productivity app: rich-text notes, tasks, a recurring-event calendar, and scheduled notifications behind a single Material Design 3 interface. Offline-first with optimistic updates.
